About Me

Hello! My name is Dipendra, and I am a passionate frontend developer and a dedicated BIM student. Ever since I started exploring web development, I have been fascinated by the ability to transform ideas into interactive, visually appealing, and user-friendly websites. My journey into the world of coding began with HTML and CSS, where I learned the fundamentals of web structure, styling, and layout. Over time, I expanded my knowledge to JavaScript, which allowed me to add interactivity and dynamic features to the websites I create. This combination of creativity and logic has always inspired me to continually learn and improve my skills. I am particularly interested in building responsive websites that work seamlessly across all devices. I enjoy leveraging technologies like Flexbox and CSS Grid to create flexible and modern layouts. Understanding user experience is one of my priorities, so I always focus on clean design, intuitive navigation, and smooth interactions. Through personal projects and coursework, I have honed my abilities to design layouts, implement interactive elements, and optimize performance for faster and more efficient websites.
